🚀 Getting Started

Step 1: Choose Your Path

You can use this website as either a Teacher/User or an Administrator.

👤 For Teachers/Users

Step 1: Register

Create your account with:

  • Full Name
  • Phone Number (10 digits)

  • Email Address
  • Password (minimum 8 characters)
📝 Register Now

Step 2: Login

After registration, you'll be redirected to login. Use your Full Name and Password to access your dashboard.

Step 3: Explore Courses

Once logged in, you can:

  • Track your progress
  • View partner schools
  • Manage your profile
