Frequently Asked Questions

What services does SAIRA ACAD provide?

+

SAIRA ACAD provides comprehensive teacher recruitment services for schools, job placement support for teachers, mentorship and training programs, certification courses, and career counseling. We bridge the gap between quality educators and educational institutions.

How quickly can you help us fill teaching positions?

+

Typically, we can provide shortlisted candidates within 3-5 business days and complete placements within 2-3 weeks, depending on the role specifics and your requirements. For urgent positions, we offer expedited services.

Is there a fee for teachers to use your services?

+

No, our job placement services are completely free for teachers. We are compensated by our partner schools. However, certification and training programs have fees, which are clearly stated on our Mentorship & Training page.

What is your screening process for teachers?

+

We conduct thorough background verification, credential validation, skills assessment, reference checks, and personality evaluations to ensure quality. Only candidates who meet our standards are recommended to schools.

Do you provide support after placement?

+

Yes, we offer post-placement support for both schools and teachers to ensure smooth integration and address any concerns during the initial months.
