Internships

Kick-Start Your Career in Education Technology

Why Intern with Us?

Our internship program is designed to give you real-world experience in the EdTech sector. You'll work on meaningful projects, learn from experienced professionals, and gain insights into how education and technology intersect.

What You'll Get:

  • Hands-on experience in education technology
  • Mentorship from industry experts
  • Certificate of completion
  • Stipend for full-time internships
  • Potential for full-time employment
  • Networking opportunities in the EdTech sector

Available Internship Areas:

  • Content Development: Create educational materials and blog posts
  • Recruitment Support: Assist with teacher-school matching
  • Digital Marketing: Social media, SEO, and content marketing
  • Software Development: Work on platform features (Full-stack/Frontend/Backend)
  • Business Development: Research and outreach to schools
  • Operations: Process optimization and data management

Eligibility:

Current students or recent graduates (within 1 year) from any recognized university. Duration: 2-6 months.

Join as Mentor / Trainer

Share Your Expertise on a Flexible Basis

Become Part of Our Expert Network

Are you an experienced educator or education professional with deep expertise in your field? Join our network of mentors and trainers to guide the next generation of teachers. This is ideal for those who want to contribute to teacher development without committing to full-time employment.

What Does a SAIRA ACAD Mentor Do?

  • Conduct training sessions on specialized teaching methods
  • Provide one-on-one mentorship to aspiring teachers
  • Deliver webinars and workshops on education best practices
  • Review and provide feedback on lesson plans and teaching strategies
  • Share real-world classroom experiences and insights

Benefits of Being a Mentor:

  • Flexible Schedule: Choose your availability and commitment level
  • Competitive Compensation: Earn per session or engagement
  • Make an Impact: Shape future educators and teaching practices
  • Expand Your Network: Connect with education professionals nationwide
  • Professional Recognition: Build your reputation as an expert
  • Access Resources: Use our platform and materials for your sessions

Who Can Apply?

  • 10+ years of teaching or education leadership experience
  • Specialized expertise in subject areas or pedagogy
  • Strong communication and presentation skills
  • Passion for developing future educators
  • Available for minimum 4 hours per month

Mentor Categories:

📚 Subject Experts

Math, Science, Languages, Social Studies specialists

🎓 Pedagogy Specialists

Teaching methods, classroom management experts

🎨 Special Education

Arts, Sports, Special needs education

👔 Education Leadership

Principals, coordinators, curriculum designers
