Hiring Tips for Schools

Expert advice for school administrators and HR professionals

1

Define Clear Job Requirements

Create detailed job descriptions that outline specific qualifications, experience levels, and teaching methodologies required.

2

Look Beyond Certifications

While credentials are important, assess candidates' passion for teaching, communication skills, adaptability, and cultural fit.

3

Conduct Demo Classes

Observe candidates teaching a real or simulated class. This reveals their teaching style and classroom management abilities.

4

Check References Thoroughly

Contact previous employers to verify experience, teaching effectiveness, and professional conduct.
